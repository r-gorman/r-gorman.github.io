Our Adoption Process & Promise

We want to make sure every match is built to last a lifetime.

1. Submit an Application

To kick off the process, you'll first submit an adoption application. One of our volunteers will review it closely. If you currently have pets, we do require proof of their current vaccinations and ongoing heartworm prevention to ensure everyone's health and safety.

2. Meet & Greet

Once your application is reviewed and approved, we'll coordinate a meet-and-greet so you can spend some one-on-one time with the dog and make sure they're the right fit for you.

3. Foster-to-Adopt (Recommended)

We highly recommend foster-to-adopt scenarios! It's a wonderful way to see how the dog settles into your actual home environment and daily routine before you make a permanent commitment.

4. Have Questions About the Process?

If you have any questions regarding our adoption requirements, scheduling, or specific dogs, we would love to hear from you. Head over to our contact page to get in touch with our team!
