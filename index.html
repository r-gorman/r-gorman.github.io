About Jak's Second Chance Rescue Ranch

Based in Cabot, AR and serving Central Arkansas, our rescue is dedicated to saving abandoned and neglected dogs, providing them with necessary medical care, rehabilitation, and placing them into safe, temporary foster environments until they find their permanent families.

Jak's Second Chance Rescue Ranch - Dog Adoption in Cabot, AR

We are a 100% volunteer-operated 501(c)(3) animal rescue ranch based in Cabot, Arkansas, serving Pulaski County, Lonoke County, and all surrounding Central Arkansas regions. We specialize in rescuing abandoned dogs, providing them with critical veterinary triage, medical care, and matching them with loving foster homes until they are ready for permanent pet adoption.

Find adoptable puppies, lab mixes, pit bull terriers, hounds, and senior rescue dogs near you. Apply to become a foster home provider or submit your formal dog adoption application today.
