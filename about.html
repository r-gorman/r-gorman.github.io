Who We Are

Jak's Second Chance Rescue Ranch is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to saving dogs throughout Central Arkansas and helping them find loving forever homes. We provide safe shelter, essential medical rehabilitation, and compassionate care for dogs that need a fresh start.

While we operate primarily as a foster-based rescue—relying on wonderful families to give our dogs a normal home life while they wait for adoption—we also maintain a small shelter building. This onsite facility gives us a dedicated space for intakes and specialized care, backed by a caretaker who lives on the property around the clock to ensure our dogs are always safe, watched over, and loved.

We are a 100% volunteer-run organization with zero paid staff. Every dollar donated and every hour volunteered goes straight to the dogs—fueling our mission, covering medical care, and keeping our rescue running.

Our Mission & Values

Every dog deserves safety, health, and a dedicated family to call their own.

Rescue & Rehabilitation

We take in dogs from local shelters and high-risk situations, providing them with complete medical checkups, proper nutrition, and behavioral support via our trusted partner veterinarians.

Foster Network

Our active foster homes play a crucial role by providing a loving family environment where our rescue dogs can decompress and learn household manners while awaiting adoption.

Lifelong Matches

We carefully screen adopters to ensure every placement is a permanent, secure match, setting both the dog and the family up for a lifetime of companionship.
