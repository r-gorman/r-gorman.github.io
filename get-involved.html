Become a Foster Hero

Fostering is one of the most impactful ways to help. By opening your home, you give a rescue dog a safe, loving place to decompress, learn household routines, and get ready for their forever family. Plus, it frees up space for us to rescue another dog in need.

We Cover Everything

When you foster with Jak's, you do not have to pay a dime (unless you want to spoil them with extra treats!). We provide all food, supplies, and fully cover medical care. Just let us know what you need, and we'll get it right to you.

Other Ways to Help

Not ready to foster? We always appreciate extra hands at our building and around the community. Reach out to ask about volunteering opportunities, help with transport, or assistance with maintenance and events.
